The dataset is shared under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). This notice applies to `data/salesdaily.csv`. The analysis code and notebook are separate from the dataset license.

The source aggregates sales into eight ATC categories and does not provide individual medicine or brand names.
